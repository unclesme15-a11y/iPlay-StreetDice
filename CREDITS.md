## Hot-Streak Dice

"Dice" ([https://skfb.ly/6UoEV](https://skfb.ly/6UoEV)) by Geug is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution ([http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).

Source file supplied by the project owner: `dice.glb`.
Use: the player's dice while the hot streak is fully active.
Integration changes: Unity import, scale/pivot and face-orientation alignment for gameplay; opaque gameplay material to prevent opposite-side pips showing through. Original source retained unchanged in `Assets/Art/HotDice/dice.glb`.
