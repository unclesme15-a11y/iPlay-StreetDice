Common questions

Is this real-money gambling?
No. Every chip and dollar amount in the game is play money. It has no cash value, can't be bought with real money, and can't be cashed out, transferred, or redeemed for anything. See the Terms of Use for the full policy.

Do I need an account?
No email or password is required to play. You're identified by an anonymous player ID and whatever display name you choose.

How does voice chat work?
Table voice chat is optional and only connects you to other players seated at your table. It's provided by Unity Gaming Services (Vivox); we don't record or store your voice.

Someone is being abusive in voice chat or in a game.
Please report it to [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] with the table/game details and roughly when it happened.

I want my data deleted.
Since play doesn't require an email or real name, most data is tied only to an anonymous device/player ID. Contact [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll help.
